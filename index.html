How it works

Screenshot mode uses browser canvas/pixel heuristics on the uploaded image. Live mode fetches candles through the proxy and computes the read directly from the OHLC series — EMA slope, swing highs/lows, and break of structure.

Data integrity

Market data can be delayed, incomplete, stale, or provider-limited. NEPSE support is experimental and relies on unofficial/community data sources.

Pricing

Free beta. No paid plan is active yet, and no brokerage execution is connected.

Before trading

Do your own verification. ChartLens does not know your goals, portfolio, risk tolerance, location, or regulatory status.
